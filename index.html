All traffic is earned organically: search-intent content written for specific legal, repair and compliance questions. Readers arrive with high commercial intent — they are comparing document services, looking for local tradespeople, or checking compliance deadlines.

How we monetise (and disclose)

Affiliate & commission links

Contextual recommendations to document services, legal-tech and home-service platforms. Our affiliate activity runs through established affiliate networks — including Awin and FlexOffers — with programme-level approval required before any tracked link goes live. Every monetised link carries rel="sponsored nofollow noopener" and sits beside a plain-language advertising disclosure meeting FTC Endorsement Guides (16 CFR Part 255) and UK ASA/CAP expectations.

Paid listings & sponsorships

Paid directory placements and featured listings are labelled as promoted and never blended into editorial rankings without a visible label.

Editorial standards

Human-edited content, verified against primary sources (statutes, court websites, official fee schedules). No scraped or auto-generated filler; corrections applied on notice; sales/advice boundaries kept clear — we publish information, not regulated advice.
